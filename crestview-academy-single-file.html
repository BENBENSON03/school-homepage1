About Us

Crestview Academy is a private school built on a simple belief: every child learns best when they feel known, challenged and supported. Our teaching blends strong academic foundations with character development, so students grow into curious thinkers and confident, responsible young people.

For over two decades we have welcomed families into a close-knit learning community. Our qualified teachers, small class sizes and caring pastoral team help students achieve strong academic results while discovering talents in sport, the arts and leadership.

Why Choose Our School

We combine quality teaching, modern learning environments, and holistic development to help every student thrive.

  • Qualified Teachers

    Our dedicated educators provide engaging instruction and personalized guidance to support every learner's progress.

  • Modern Facilities

    Our learning spaces and educational resources are designed to encourage curiosity, collaboration, and practical learning.

  • Student Support

    We encourage students to develop confidence, build positive relationships, and seek guidance whenever they need it.

  • Extracurricular Activities

    Sports, clubs, creative activities, and leadership opportunities help students discover their talents beyond the classroom.

  • Academic Excellence

    We promote strong academic foundations, critical thinking, discipline, and a lifelong passion for learning.
